FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our funeral cover.

Do you charge a joining fee?

No. KFG Funeral Service does not charge a joining fee.

What is the waiting period?

The standard waiting period is six months. Members transferring from another funeral cover with valid proof may qualify for no waiting period.

Can I cover my family?

Yes. Our Family Cover allows a main member to cover up to five dependants.

Do you offer group funeral cover?

Yes. Churches, choirs, organisations and community groups can enquire about our Group Cover.

How do I join KFG Funeral Service?

Click the Join Now button and complete the membership application, or contact KFG directly for assistance.
